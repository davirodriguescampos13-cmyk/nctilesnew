03 / Our Story

From Portugal
to the USA

PortugalBrazilUSA

Hello, we are NC Tiles and Remodeling. We have over 25 years of experience working with tile — from large commercial installations to the smallest residential repairs — and we bring that same craftsmanship to every project we take on.

Our journey started in Portugal, where we learned the traditional techniques of stone and ceramic work that still shape how we approach every job today. From there we spent years working across Brazil, mastering porcelain and marble installations in one of the most demanding construction markets in the world.

Today we are located in the USA, and we are here to serve all of you — homeowners, contractors and designers — with the same precision, honesty and care that built our reputation overseas. Whether it's a single bathroom or a full home remodel, we treat every project as a reflection of our name.
